FAQs

Is it really free?

Yes, for homes and small businesses. For multifamily buildings and portfolios, email contact@cohi.energy or use the form below to set up a portfolio review.

Do I have to change how I use energy?

No, that’s the point. We work on the plan and setup behind your bill, not on how much energy you use.

Doesn’t my utility already do this?

Kind of. There is a comparison tool on their site, and it tries. But helping you pay less for the same usage was never really the utility’s job. We’ve made it ours.

Their version gives each plan one number and no explanation. It also won’t tell you which plans you actually qualify for.

Cohi shows how every option plays out on your real usage, month by month, and explains each one in plain language.

Will switching plans affect my service?

No. Your utility, wires, meter, and reliability stay exactly the same; only your billing changes.

Do you work for an energy supplier?

No. We work for you. Cohi doesn’t sell energy and doesn’t take commissions from suppliers.

Which utilities do you support?

PG&E, starting with electric accounts. Gas support is coming soon.
